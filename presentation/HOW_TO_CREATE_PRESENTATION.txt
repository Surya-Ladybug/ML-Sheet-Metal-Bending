  HOW TO CREATE THE POWERPOINT PRESENTATION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

STEP 1: Open PowerPoint

STEP 2: Choose a Professional Template
  - Recommended: "Ion" or "Facet" (built-in Office templates)
  - Or download academic template from your university

STEP 3: Create 13 Slides
  - Use PRESENTATION_SLIDES_CONTENT.txt as your guide
  - Copy-paste content from each slide section
  - Add visuals as suggested

STEP 4: Design Tips
  ✓ Use consistent fonts (Arial or Calibri, 24pt minimum)
  ✓ Limit text per slide (6-7 lines maximum)
  ✓ Use diagrams/charts instead of text where possible
  ✓ Add your university logo to title slide
  ✓ Use professional color scheme (blues/grays)

STEP 5: Visual Elements to Add

  Slide 2: Timeline graphic (FEM hours vs ML milliseconds)
  Slide 3: Side-by-side comparison chart
  Slide 4: Flow diagram (copy from content file)
  Slide 5: Data table screenshot
  Slide 6: Feature importance bar chart
  Slide 7: Inverse problem diagram
  Slide 8: Interface screenshot (take from running app)
  Slide 9: Bar chart (speed comparison, log scale)
  Slide 10: Demo screenshot or video embed
  Slide 11: Checkmark list with icons
  Slide 12: Roadmap timeline
  Slide 13: Thank you graphic

STEP 6: Save As
  - Save as: PRESENTATION.pptx
  - Also save as PDF for distribution
